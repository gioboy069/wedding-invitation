For Our Guests

Dress Code Inspiration

We invite you to dress in your finest. Here are a few looks that capture the spirit of our celebration.

For Her

Floor-length gowns or elegant cocktail dresses in jewel tones, sage, sea blue, or soft botanical hues.

For Him

Dark tailored suits or tuxedos. Bow ties, pocket squares, and polished dress shoes warmly welcomed.

Garden Romance

Think refined evening wear with a touch of vineyard warmth — layers for cool Napa evenings.

Please Keep in Mind

  • White, ivory, and pale cream are reserved for the bride
  • The ceremony and reception are outdoors on grass and stone — choose footwear accordingly
  • Napa evenings can be cool in June — a wrap or jacket is a lovely idea
  • When in doubt, lean formal — we'd rather you feel overdressed than underdressed
