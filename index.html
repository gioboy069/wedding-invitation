For Our Guests

Dress Code Inspiration

We invite you to dress in your finest — floor-length gowns or elegant cocktail dresses, dark tailored suits or tuxedos, with a calm coastal mood for the evening.

  • For Her Gowns or cocktail dresses in deep ocean, seafoam, sand, or soft sky
  • For Him Dark suits or tuxedos; bow ties and pocket squares welcome
  • Evening Refined layers for cool Napa nights

Please Keep in Mind

  • White, ivory, and pale cream are reserved for the bride
  • The ceremony and reception are outdoors on grass and stone — choose footwear accordingly
  • Napa evenings can be cool in June — a wrap or jacket is a lovely idea
  • When in doubt, lean formal — we'd rather you feel overdressed than underdressed
