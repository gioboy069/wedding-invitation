Download Project

Latest wedding invitation for Render’s 512 MB plan. Photos are resized and videos are prepared one at a time, so a share does not run the server out of memory. Each RSVP is saved on the site and copied to Google Drive, and a restart loads those replies back. Sharing keeps the original clarity, shows a small circle at the side, and the Share button stays quiet until that upload finishes. On an iPhone, the photo checkmarks stay tappable while another phone is uploading or a laptop is downloading. Share sits above the guest album and opens in a popup, and the “someone shared a photo” note is sent from the guest’s browser. View more opens the rest of the photo line. A download button asks whether to continue, then shows one shaded bar with the percent inside it, and a cancel button. In Admin → Details, the ceremony and venue photos can be dragged, shifted, and cropped. Every page sends the browser security headers a scanner looks for.
